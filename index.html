Primary Teachers for 2027

Muhyiddin School is inviting applications from qualified and experienced individuals for the position of Primary Teacher for the 2027 academic year. Requirements: Relevant teaching qualification. Good communication and classroom management skills. Previous teaching experience will be an advantage. Applicants must be committed, responsible, and able to work effectively as part of a team. Application Deadline: Interested candidates are requested to submit their applications and required documents before 30 September 2026. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for an interview. For further information, please contact the school administration.

About Us

Muhyiddin School is named after the king, Al Sultan Mohamed Muhyiddeenul A’dil Siri Naakiree Sundhara Mahaaradhun, who ruled the country from 1691 to 1692. He was an eminent scholar and a prominent intellectual who contributed a great deal to improve the education and religious awareness in the country.

Our Facilities

FACILITY

Classrooms

Our fully air-conditioned classrooms are equipped with modern TVs and designed with plenty of natural light. Muhyiddin classrooms provide a calm, comfortable, and engaging environment where students can focus, learn, and excel, while teachers can deliver lessons effectively using modern teaching resources.

Vocational Lab

Our fully equipped Vocational Lab provides students with a practical learning environment designed to develop essential cooking and life skills. The lab features a modern kitchen, essential equipment, and appropriate safety facilities, allowing students to learn confidently while gaining valuable hands-on experience.
