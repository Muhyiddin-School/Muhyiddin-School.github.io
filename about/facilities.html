Our Facilities

About Us / Our Facilities
FACILITY

Classrooms

Our fully air-conditioned classrooms are equipped with modern TVs and designed with plenty of natural light. Muhyiddin classrooms provide a calm, comfortable, and engaging environment where students can focus, learn, and excel, while teachers can deliver lessons effectively using modern teaching resources.


FACILITY

Vocational Lab

Our fully equipped Vocational Lab provides students with a practical learning environment designed to develop essential cooking and life skills. The lab features a modern kitchen, essential equipment, and appropriate safety facilities, allowing students to learn confidently while gaining valuable hands-on experience.


FACILITY

Computer Lab

Our Computer Lab is equipped with up-to-date computers, high-speed internet, and a wide range of hardware for hands-on experimentation. It provides students with a modern environment to develop their digital skills, explore technology, and gain practical experience.


FACILITY

Science Lab

Our well-equipped Science Lab supports learning across Chemistry, Biology, and Physics, with the necessary equipment and safety facilities for practical exploration. Students can investigate scientific concepts, conduct experiments, and strengthen their understanding of the sciences in a safe and engaging environment.


FACILITY

Library

Our spacious Library offers a diverse collection of books and resources in a comfortable and welcoming setting. With a variety of seating options, including cozy sofas and dedicated reading spaces, students can enjoy a quiet environment for reading, research, and independent study.


FACILITY

Meeting Room

Our Meeting Room offers a professional and comfortable setting for official meetings, discussions, and collaborative sessions. Equipped with a TV for presentations and visual support, it serves as a versatile space for both teachers and students.


FACILITY

AV Room

Our Meeting Room provides a professional and comfortable setting for official meetings, discussions, and collaborative sessions. Equipped with a TV for presentations and visual communication, it serves as a versatile space for both teachers and students.


FACILITY

Health Room

Our Health Room provides a dedicated space for students to receive care and attention when needed. Equipped with two beds, wheelchairs, and essential first-aid supplies, the room is supported by an experienced health teacher to ensure students’ comfort, safety, and well-being.


FACILITY

Counsellor Room

Our Counsellor Room provides a comfortable, welcoming, and trusted space where students can speak openly and seek guidance. Supported by two highly skilled counsellors, the room offers a private and confidential environment for students of all ages, from the youngest learners to senior students.


FACILITY

Garden

Our Garden offers a peaceful outdoor space where students can reconnect with nature, unwind, and enjoy a refreshing change of surroundings. It provides a welcoming setting to relax, spend time with friends, and make the most of their free time in a calm and pleasant environment.


FACILITY

Turf Compound

Our well-maintained, fully turfed football field provides an ideal space for students to participate in sports, physical activities, and outdoor recreation. It encourages an active lifestyle while offering a safe and spacious setting for training, games, and school sporting events.


FACILITY

Veli Compound

Our spacious sand compound provides a versatile outdoor area for a wide range of activities, including handball, badminton, football, camping, and recreational games. With ample open space, it offers students an enjoyable setting for sports, outdoor learning, and leisure activities.


FACILITY

Old Hall

Our fully equipped Old Hall is a versatile space designed for training programmes, small to medium-sized ceremonies, gatherings, and school events. With a dedicated sound system and a spacious layout, it provides a suitable setting for presentations, programmes, and community occasions.


FACILITY

New Hall

Our spacious New Hall is a versatile venue designed to accommodate indoor sports, large ceremonies, concerts, and major school events. With its generous capacity and open layout, it provides an ideal setting for bringing the school community together for memorable occasions.
